Consultant scoring

The sales funnel for every consultant, ranked. Enquiries → test drives → bookings → retails → deliveries.

Enquiries, test drives and bookings are typed in. Retails and deliveries are counted from the cars automatically. The Funnel score is the weighted conversion across the five stages. Home visits count with test drives.

Daily entry

Enter each consultant’s numbers for the day. Daily entries add up to the month’s funnel total (attendance stays monthly). Retails & deliveries come from cars automatically.
